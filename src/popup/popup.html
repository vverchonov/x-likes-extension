Data this extension collects

Nothing is collected until you agree. ScrollX does not watch X, save your account, or send a like before that.

After you agree, liking, reposting, or commenting on a post on X sends that post:

  • Your X username, profile picture, and numeric account ID (required to send an observation)
  • The post text, images, videos, and link
  • The time you liked, reposted, or commented

A reply to a comment under a post still sends the post, not the comment. Unlikes and removing a repost are not collected. Your signed-in X account is saved on this device. An exportable Ed25519 signing key is stored locally in the extension; its public key and signed requests identify this installation to the backend. Back up the private key to restore the same history on another installation. This key is not a payout wallet. A wallet address is sent only if you later claim a payout. You can turn capture off in settings.
