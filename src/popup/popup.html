Data this extension collects

Nothing is collected until you agree. ScrollX does not watch X, save your account, or send a like before that.

After you agree, liking, reposting, or commenting on a post on X sends that post:

  • Your X username and profile picture
  • The post text, images, videos, and link
  • The time you liked, reposted, or commented

A reply to a comment under a post still sends the post, not the comment. Unlikes and removing a repost are not collected. Your signed-in X username is saved on this device so this popup can show that account’s likes and earnings. A wallet address is sent only if you later claim a payout.
